About Me

I'm a student from Çerkezköy, Türkiye, completing both the Turkish high school curriculum and the Ontario Secondary School Diploma. Mathematics is where my curiosity started; computer science and artificial intelligence are where I want it to lead.

Outside the classroom I compete in school sports, represent a country as a Model UN delegate and volunteer with people with disabilities. Each of these has taught me something I want to bring to a university community: discipline, collaboration and responsibility.
